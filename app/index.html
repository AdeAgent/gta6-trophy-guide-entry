Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No difficulty settings, no mission replay, no New Game+, and free roam continues after the story, so the order below is about comfort, not survival: story first, side lines while they are open, the one possibly-missable meditation early, then the 100% checklist, strangers and money last.

Missables — what can actually go wrong

Good news first: there is no mission replay in GTA VI, but story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed, and both PowerPyx and GTABase flag only one trophy as possibly missable. The honest watch list under it is drawn from the guides, not guessed.

Sources & honesty

Every guide in this app names where its facts come from, with the link, the date we read it (2026-11-19) and what it backs.
